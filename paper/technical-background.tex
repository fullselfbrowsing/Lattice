\section{Technical Background and Comparison}\label{app:technical-background}

The following comparison uses the cited preprints as accessed on November 5, 2026; it is not a survey of every current release. \emph{TrustTrack} is a vision paper that argues for verifiable identity, policy commitments, and tamper-resistant behavioral logs in multi-agent systems \citep{li2025trusttrack}. \emph{Sello} (``Notarized Agents'') inverts the trust boundary: the service that receives an agent's call signs a receipt, encrypts it to the agent's owner, and publishes it to a witness-cosigned Merkle log, reducing reliance on the agent or its operator while retaining the receiver and log assumptions \citep{figuera2026sello}. \emph{Janus} places a signed, hash-chained log on the effect path of agentic LLMs, so that a step's proposal and verdict are durable before its effect is released. Its gates are pure functions of the log and can be re-derived offline, and it is evaluated under crash injection and with a lending workflow \citep{arslan2026janus}. Two further systems are run in the comparison of Section~\ref{sec:realworld} but are not tabulated here: Agent Flight Recorder, which keeps tamper-evident audit trails of tool-using agents with on-chain anchoring \citep{bindschaedler2026afr}, and NovaFabric, which seals tamper-evident, replayable evidence for autonomous agent runs \citep{ardebili2026novafabric}.

Table~\ref{tab:compare} compares these systems with the artifact presented here. Lattice combines issuer-side receipts for signed calls, terminal single-agent results, and crews; deterministic routing with a signed selected route; redaction before signing; and content commitments. Lattice still lacks Sello's receiver-side attestation and transparency log and Janus's effect gating and crash-consistency evaluation. Its current optional task, policy, and request bindings remain issuer claims (Section~\ref{sec:artifact}). The project repository records receipt type definitions and signing dependencies \citep{lattice} in commit \texttt{fa6d5a1}, dated 11 May 2026, before the Sello (June 2026) and Janus (September 2026) preprints appeared. Audience, nonce, and expiry binding, chain verification, chain closure, and the later execution-binding extensions were added in October 2026, after both. This repository chronology does not establish independent invention or priority over the wider literature.
