## Submission Readiness Notes

- Do not submit or circulate beyond the professor before IP/patent review.
- Use Independent Researcher and personal Gmail unless the professor confirms a
  UTD coauthor or affiliation path.
- Avoid employer affiliation and employer email.
- Keep the manuscript academically cautious: use "tamper-evident"; prefer "we
  contribute" and "we integrate" over unsupported novelty-superlative claims.
- Before submission, adapt formatting to the selected journal's author
  guidelines.
