## Search and selection

The starting set was the manuscript's governance, accountability, auditing, provenance/security and DSR bibliography. Searches followed the three additions specified in the approved C6 plan and sought publisher or author-repository copies of the existing core works. Search scope comprised AIS eLibrary/MIS Quarterly, journal publisher pages, university/author repositories and arXiv for explicitly identified technical preprints. Crossref was used for metadata checks, not for claims about findings. No subscription database export, reproducible exhaustive hit count, PRISMA flow or independent screening was performed.

Representative exact queries used during discovery/retrieval:

- `"Guardrails for Human-AI Ecologies" pdf DOI`

- `"Responsible artificial intelligence governance" "101885" pdf ntnu`
- `"Artificial Intelligence Governance For Businesses" arxiv`

- `"zkLLM: Zero Knowledge Proofs for Large Language Models" arxiv`
- `"Positioning and Presenting Design Science Research" improvement exaptation Gregor Hevner pdf`

Inclusion required a direct contribution to organizational responsibility, delegation/oversight, evidence for review, secure records, or evaluation of design knowledge. Prefer primary journal texts for the IS argument; retain relevant technical preprints as preprints and separate their claimed mechanisms from measured comparisons. The automation–augmentation paper by Raisch/Krakowski was considered during planning but not added: the three selected IS additions more directly support the agreed delegation/oversight focus. General AI adoption surveys, commercial governance advice, secondary paper summaries and unrelated search hits were not used as evidence. Citation count and venue prestige were not inclusion tests.

Full text was read at the relevant claim/limitation passages where accessible. Three core works remain abstract/overview-only in this pass; their use is deliberately bounded below. Author reading and synthesis are still open. Downloaded PDFs and metadata responses are private research copies under `.context/jitcar-c6/sources/`; do not include them in the submission archive. `retrievals*.json` and `manifest.json` distinguish successful local bytes from failed/HTML responses. Some full texts were readable through the browser's indexed PDF service even when a direct download failed; these are identified explicitly, not represented as local PDFs.
