\subsection{Separate zero-cost hosted cohort}

A separate follow-up recorded 428/428 planned cases, excluding 12 latency warmups. It used the free catalog variants of Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and Dots 3 Note Preview. Model and endpoint prices were checked as zero before inference; model fallback and server-side extensions were disallowed. Provider-reported charges across development, evaluation and judging total USD 0.000000 over 784 requests; 50 requests lack a reported cost. The earlier paid ledger and its unresolved reservations were preserved.

These results are not pooled with the paid cohort. An initial development smoke found Gemma 4 31B rate-limited and small output allowances exhausted by Nemotron reasoning. Before heldout inference, the second model was changed to Dots and output caps were fixed at 1024 tokens for simple tasks and 4096 for citations and agents, with temperature zero and provider-default reasoning. Development recordings remain separate.

Both citation arms use the same 60 frozen ALCE-derived questions and retrieved passages. 120/120 completed citation request pairs had identical body hashes. The contract validates syntax and citation identifiers after generation; it does not repair answers or establish entailment. Differences in generated quality between arms therefore do not demonstrate that the contract improved generation.

The fixed free Nemotron 3 Ultra judge is exploratory and has not been calibrated against human annotations. These support judgments are neither human accuracy labels nor official ALCE scores. Invalid or unavailable answers remain in the 60-question denominator. An initial Xing judge attempt was rate-limited and is retained separately, not pooled. The replacement passed two synthetic supported/contradicted probes. It uses temperature zero, a 4096-token output cap and a 1024-token reasoning allowance. Judge replies may have one outer JSON fence removed; candidate answers are unchanged. Independent judgments use a shared rate limiter and at most four concurrent requests; collector hashes record the scheduling transition.

Overlapping judge failure labels include 31 answers with an irrelevant citation, 14 incorrect answers, 9 answers with an uncited claim, and 8 with an unsupported claim. These counts overlap and are not human-confirmed labels.

Post-hoc format diagnostics found 8 empty citation lists, 8 empty responses, 1 invalid JSON outputs, 2 unavailable responses, 8 unused source identifiers. Among empty responses, 6 ended at the token limit and 2 were filtered. These diagnoses do not change scores. Empty citation lists are not automatically justified abstentions; filtered outputs are not JSON-repair opportunities.
